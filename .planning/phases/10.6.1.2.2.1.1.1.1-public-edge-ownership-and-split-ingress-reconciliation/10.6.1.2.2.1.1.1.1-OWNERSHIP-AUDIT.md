## Short Report

The project now has one exact public-edge ownership map for `77.66.186.75`.

Simple explanation:

- the preserved Windows host `192.168.88.250` is alive on LAN and still serves `Caddy`
- the Linux relay host `77.66.186.75` is alive and still serves local relay traffic through `nginx`
- but the real public IP currently belongs to the Ubuntu-side edge, not to the preserved Windows edge
- the public responder is therefore split between:
  - Ubuntu `nginx` on the real public IP
  - Windows `Caddy` on the LAN host only

That means the public contract is still not owned by one deliberate layer.
