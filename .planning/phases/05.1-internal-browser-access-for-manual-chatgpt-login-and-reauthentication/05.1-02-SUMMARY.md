## Decisions Made

- Keep browser access sessions in-memory and worker-scoped because the access window is operator-only and intentionally short-lived.
- Reuse the same browser access lifecycle for first login and reauth instead of maintaining separate recovery mechanics.
- Keep viewer traffic on the internal edge and proxy directly to worker-local noVNC endpoints instead of exposing raw worker ports.
