## Scope

This phase is valid only if it makes router truth and public-ingress truth explicit.

| Requirement | Covered By | Expected Evidence |
|-------------|------------|------------------|
| MTIK-01 | 10.6.1.1-01 | router/server audit artifact with services, interfaces, and ingress truth |
| MTIK-02 | 10.6.1.1-02 | deliberate NAT/forward path to `192.168.88.2` or an exact router-side blocker |

## Verification Gates

### Gate 1: Router Truth

- the phase records which router boundary sits in front of `192.168.88.2`
- the audit shows current service exposure and current ingress symptoms
- the phase distinguishes server-local health from router/WAN ingress

### Gate 2: NAT and Hardening Truth

- one deliberate NAT strategy exists for the relay edge
- router admin exposure is addressed or a precise auth blocker is recorded
- the server target is the deliberate `nginx` edge, not the raw relay socket

### Gate 3: External Proof

- an outside-client check was run against the intended public path
- the result is explicit: usable or blocked
- if blocked, the blocker is precise and router/public-edge specific

Do not mark this phase complete unless router truth, NAT truth, and outside proof are all explicit.

- router audit is explicit
- the NAT/hardening intent is explicit
